# NimbusWorks Travel Booking SOP

**Version 1.2 | Effective: January 1, 2024**

## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes the process for booking business travel at NimbusWorks. It applies to all employees travelling for work-related purposes including client meetings, conferences, and inter-office travel.

## 2. Pre-Travel Approval

All business travel must be approved **before booking**. The approval process is:

1. Submit a Travel Request in the HR portal (hr.nimbusworks.com → Travel → New Request) at least **10 business days** before the travel date
2. Include: destination, dates, purpose, estimated total cost
3. Requests under $500 require manager approval only
4. Requests between $500 and $2,000 require manager + Director approval
5. Requests over $2,000 require VP approval

Emergency travel (less than 10 days notice) requires email approval from the manager, to be followed up in the HR portal within 3 business days.

## 3. Booking Channels

Employees must use the **NimbusWorks Travel Portal** (powered by Navan) for all flights, trains, and hotels. Booking outside the portal requires prior approval from Finance and may delay reimbursement.

The travel portal automatically enforces:
- Hotel rate caps ($200/night domestic, $300/night international)
- Flight class restrictions (economy under 6 hours, business 6+ hours)
- Preferred suppliers with negotiated rates

## 4. Flights

Book the most economical fare that:
- Allows changes and cancellations (refundable or flexible fares preferred)
- Departs at a reasonable time (avoid flights before 6:00 AM or after 11:00 PM unless no alternatives exist)

Avoid checking bags where possible (use carry-on luggage for trips under 5 days). Checked baggage fees are reimbursable for trips of 5+ days.

## 5. Accommodation

Book accommodations through the travel portal. Hotels must be within the rate limits set in the **Expense Policy**. Exceptions require pre-approval and must be documented with a reason (e.g., no rooms available within the cap at the required location).

Early check-in or late checkout fees are reimbursable if required for business reasons.

## 6. Ground Transport

For airport transfers:
- Use rideshare (Uber, Lyft) or taxi
- Car rentals must be pre-approved (compact or intermediate class only)
- Personal vehicle use at $0.67 per mile

For local transport at destination:
- Public transit preferred where practical
- Rideshare acceptable

## 7. International Travel

For international trips:
- Notify HR at least **3 weeks in advance** for visa/travel document verification
- Check travel advisories at travel.state.gov
- Register your travel with IT security (security@nimbusworks.com) for extended trips (over 5 days)
- Purchase travel insurance (available through the travel portal, company-covered)

Employees with dual citizenship or complex visa requirements should contact HR at least **6 weeks in advance**.

## 8. Expense Submission

Submit all travel expenses within **30 days** of return (see Expense Policy for full details). Attach:
- Airline boarding passes or e-tickets
- Hotel folio (final bill, not just booking confirmation)
- Receipts for all expenses over $25
- Ground transport receipts

## 9. Cancellations

Cancel approved travel as soon as plans change. Use the travel portal to cancel flights and hotels. Unused refundable fares are credited back to the company card automatically. Non-refundable expenses require a cancellation report in the HR portal.
