# NimbusWorks HR Policy

**Version 3.2 | Effective: January 1, 2024**

## 1. Employment Terms

All full-time employees at NimbusWorks are employed on a permanent basis after successfully completing the probation period. The standard probation period is **3 months** from the date of joining. During probation, either party may terminate employment with 7 days written notice. After probation, the standard notice period is **30 days** for all employees below the Vice President level. Vice Presidents and above are required to provide **90 days** notice.

Part-time and contract employees are governed by the terms specified in their individual contracts.

## 2. Working Hours

The standard working week at NimbusWorks is **40 hours**, typically Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM in the employee's local timezone. Flexible working hours are permitted with explicit manager approval, subject to the requirement that employees are available during **core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM**.

Overtime is not expected as a routine part of the job. Any overtime in excess of 10 hours per week requires prior approval from the employee's department head. Overtime compensation for hourly employees follows applicable local labor law.

## 3. Performance Reviews

NimbusWorks conducts formal performance reviews **twice per year**: in **June** (mid-year check-in) and **December** (annual review). The December review determines year-end compensation adjustments, bonuses, and promotions.

Employees who receive a rating of 2 or below (on a 1–5 scale) in any two consecutive reviews may be placed on a **90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**. Failure to meet PIP objectives may result in termination of employment.

## 4. Compensation

Salaries are reviewed annually as part of the December performance review cycle. Market adjustment increases are typically **3% to 7%** for employees rated "Meets Expectations" (3) or above. Employees rated 5 ("Outstanding") are eligible for additional merit increases and spot bonuses at management discretion.

Salary information is confidential. Employees must not disclose their compensation to colleagues.

## 5. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct. Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination. Employees should refer to the separate **Code of Conduct Policy** for full details.

## 6. Grievance Procedure

Employees with workplace concerns should first raise the matter with their direct manager. If the issue is not resolved within **5 business days**, the employee may escalate to HR at hr@nimbusworks.com. For serious complaints involving managers, employees may contact HR directly.

## 7. Termination

Upon termination (voluntary or involuntary), the employee must return all company equipment, revoke access credentials, and complete an exit interview within the final week of employment. Final pay including accrued unused leave is processed within **7 business days** of the final working day.
