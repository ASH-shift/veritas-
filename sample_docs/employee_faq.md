# NimbusWorks Employee FAQ

**Last Updated: March 2024**

This document answers common questions from new and existing NimbusWorks employees.

---

## Leave and Time Off

**Q: How many vacation days do I get?**
A: Full-time employees receive **18 days of paid annual leave** per year. Leave accrues at 1.5 days per month. New hires receive prorated leave based on their start date.

**Q: Can I carry over unused vacation?**
A: Yes, you can carry forward a maximum of **5 days** to the following year. Carried-forward days must be used by March 31 of the following year or they are forfeited. Days beyond 5 at year-end are forfeited automatically.

**Q: How much sick leave do I have?**
A: Employees receive **10 days of sick leave** per year. Sick leave resets on January 1 and does not carry forward. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

**Q: When does maternity leave apply?**
A: Employees who have worked at NimbusWorks for at least **12 months** are entitled to **26 weeks of paid maternity leave**. This applies to biological and adoptive mothers.

## Compensation and Benefits

**Q: When are salaries reviewed?**
A: Salary reviews happen during the **December Annual Performance Review**. Changes take effect February 1 of the following year.

**Q: What is the 401(k) match?**
A: NimbusWorks matches **100% of your contribution up to 4%** of your gross salary. The match vests immediately from day one. You are auto-enrolled at a 3% contribution rate.

**Q: Is there a learning budget?**
A: Yes! Every employee receives **$1,000 per year** for learning and development. This covers courses, books, conferences, and certifications. Pre-approval is needed for items over $200.

**Q: Does the company pay for the gym?**
A: NimbusWorks reimburses up to **$50 per month** for gym memberships or fitness-related subscriptions. Submit receipts through Expensify under the "Wellness Benefit" category.

**Q: What life insurance does the company provide?**
A: NimbusWorks provides basic life insurance coverage of **3 times your annual base salary** at no cost to you.

## Remote Work

**Q: How many days do I need to come into the office?**
A: The hybrid policy requires **3 days per week** in the office. The preferred days are Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.

**Q: Is there an allowance for working from home?**
A: Remote and hybrid employees receive **$50 per month** as an internet allowance, paid in payroll. New remote employees also get a **$1,500 one-time equipment stipend** for home office setup.

**Q: Can I work from another country?**
A: You can work remotely from outside your country of employment for up to **20 consecutive days** without special approval. Longer periods require HR and Legal approval, requested at least 6 weeks in advance.

## Expenses and Travel

**Q: What is the meal per diem for domestic travel?**
A: The per diem for domestic travel is **$50 per day** for meals. For international travel, it is **$80 per day**.

**Q: Do I need receipts for expenses?**
A: Yes, receipts are required for any expense over **$25**. Submit expenses within **30 days** of incurring them.

**Q: What class can I fly?**
A: Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights **6 hours or longer**.

## IT and Security

**Q: How long should my password be?**
A: Passwords must be at least **12 characters** and include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character. Passwords must be changed every **90 days**.

**Q: Is MFA required?**
A: Yes. Multi-factor authentication has been **mandatory for all employees since January 1, 2023**.

**Q: Do I need a VPN to work remotely?**
A: Yes. All access to internal NimbusWorks systems from outside the office **must use the VPN** (vpn.nimbusworks.com).

---

## Performance Reviews

**Q: When are performance reviews?**
A: Performance reviews are conducted **twice a year: June (mid-year) and December (annual)**. The December review determines compensation changes.

**Q: What is the rating scale?**
A: Ratings are on a **1 to 5 scale**: 1 = Needs Improvement, 2 = Developing, 3 = Meets Expectations, 4 = Exceeds Expectations, 5 = Outstanding.

**Q: What happens if I get a low rating?**
A: Employees who receive a rating of 2 or below in two consecutive reviews may be placed on a **90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**. Failure to meet PIP objectives can lead to termination.
