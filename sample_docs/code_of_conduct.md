# NimbusWorks Code of Conduct

**Version 2.0 | Effective: January 1, 2024**

## 1. Our Commitment

NimbusWorks is committed to maintaining a workplace that is respectful, inclusive, and free from discrimination and harassment. This Code applies to all employees, contractors, and anyone acting on behalf of NimbusWorks, in all work-related settings including offices, remote work, travel, and company events.

## 2. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

NimbusWorks has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, religion, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

Harassment includes but is not limited to:
- Verbal abuse, offensive jokes, or derogatory comments
- Unwelcome physical contact
- Persistent unwanted communication
- Displaying offensive materials

Violations will result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any real or potential conflicts of interest to their manager and HR. Conflicts include:
- Outside employment or consulting for a competitor
- Financial interest in a vendor or customer
- Personal relationships with direct reports or those in the hiring chain

Employees with undisclosed conflicts of interest may face termination.

## 4. Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may accept gifts of a nominal value (under **$50**) from vendors or customers. Gifts valued at $50 or more **must be reported** to the employee's manager and recorded in the Gifts Register maintained by Finance.

Cash gifts, gift cards, or gifts that could be construed as a bribe are never acceptable regardless of value.

## 5. Bribery and Anti-Corruption

NimbusWorks has a **zero-tolerance policy for bribery** in any form. Employees may not offer, pay, request, or accept bribes, kickbacks, or facilitation payments. This applies to interactions with government officials, customers, vendors, and partners.

## 6. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

Employees must protect NimbusWorks confidential information, customer data, and intellectual property. This obligation continues after employment ends. Employees may not use company IP for personal benefit or share it with third parties without authorization.

## 7. Social Media

Employees are free to use social media but must not:
- Disclose confidential company information
- Make statements that could be perceived as official NimbusWorks positions without authorization
- Post content that violates this Code of Conduct

## 8. Reporting Violations

Employees who witness or experience a violation of this Code should report it:
- **Email**: hr@nimbusworks.com
- **Anonymous hotline**: +1-800-555-0177 (available 24/7)
- **Anonymous web form**: ethics.nimbusworks.com

Reports are treated confidentially. NimbusWorks strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report.

## 9. Consequences

Violations of this Code are taken seriously. Disciplinary action ranges from a formal warning to immediate termination, depending on the severity. NimbusWorks may also pursue legal remedies where applicable.
