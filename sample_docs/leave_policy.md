# NimbusWorks Leave Policy

**Version 2.5 | Effective: January 1, 2024**

## 1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to **18 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per month. New employees joining mid-year receive prorated leave based on their joining date.

Employees may carry forward a maximum of **5 unused leave days** to the following calendar year. Carried-forward leave expires on March 31 of the following year. Any unused days beyond 5 at year-end are forfeited.

Annual leave must be requested at least **3 business days in advance** for leaves of up to 3 days, and at least **2 weeks in advance** for leaves of 4 or more consecutive days.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to **10 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not carry forward and resets on January 1 each year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave exceeding **3 consecutive days**.

Sick leave may not be taken immediately before or after annual leave without a valid medical certificate.

## 3. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to **6 days of casual leave** per year for personal emergencies or unforeseen circumstances. Casual leave should be reported to the manager on the day of absence or as soon as practically possible. Casual leave does not carry forward.

## 4. Maternity Leave

Employees who have completed at least **12 months of continuous service** are entitled to **26 weeks of paid maternity leave**. This applies to biological and adoptive mothers. An additional 4 weeks of unpaid leave may be requested with management approval.

Employees returning from maternity leave are guaranteed the same or equivalent position.

## 5. Paternity Leave

Male employees and non-birth parents are entitled to **2 weeks of paid paternity leave** following the birth or adoption of a child. Paternity leave must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption finalization.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to **3 paid days** for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). **1 paid day** is provided for extended family (grandparents, in-laws). Additional unpaid leave may be granted at manager discretion.

## 7. Public Holidays

NimbusWorks observes all public holidays applicable in the employee's country of employment. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off to be taken within 30 days.

## 8. Leave Without Pay

Employees may apply for Leave Without Pay (LWP) for up to **30 days per year** for personal reasons not covered by other leave categories. LWP requests require HR and department head approval and must be submitted at least 1 month in advance except in emergencies.

## 9. Contact

All leave requests are processed through the HR portal at hr.nimbusworks.com. Questions may be directed to hr@nimbusworks.com.
