# NimbusWorks Performance Review SOP

**Audience: All Managers and Employees | Version: 2.1 | Last Updated: January 2024**

## 1. Review Cycle

NimbusWorks conducts formal performance reviews **twice per year**:
- **Mid-Year Review (June)**: Feedback-focused check-in, no compensation decisions
- **Annual Review (December)**: Full review with compensation, promotion, and PIP decisions

The review cycle opens on the **1st of the review month** and closes on the **28th**. Results are communicated to employees by the last business day of the month.

## 2. Rating Scale

All employees are rated on a **1–5 scale**:

| Rating | Label | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **5** | Outstanding | Consistently exceeds all expectations; ready for the next level |
| **4** | Exceeds Expectations | Regularly exceeds most goals; high performer |
| **3** | Meets Expectations | Achieves all key goals; solid performer |
| **2** | Developing | Partially meets expectations; improvement needed |
| **1** | Needs Improvement | Significantly below expectations; immediate action required |

The expected distribution is roughly: 5% at rating 5, 20% at rating 4, 60% at rating 3, 12% at rating 4, and 3% at rating 1.

## 3. Review Process

### Self-Assessment
Employees complete a self-assessment in the HR portal by the **7th of the review month**. The self-assessment covers:
- Goal achievement (weight: 60%)
- Core competencies (weight: 30%)
- Culture and values (weight: 10%)

### Manager Review
Managers complete their assessments by the **15th of the review month**. Managers should gather feedback from at least 2 peer colleagues (360-degree feedback).

### Calibration
Directors and VPs conduct calibration sessions between the **15th and 22nd** to ensure consistency across teams. Calibration ensures that rating distributions are fair and comparable across departments.

### Feedback Meeting
Managers must conduct a 1:1 feedback meeting with each direct report by the **28th**. The meeting should be at least 30 minutes and cover:
- Rating and rationale
- Highlights and areas for development
- Goals for the next review period
- Compensation changes (annual review only)

## 4. Compensation Adjustments (Annual Review Only)

Compensation changes take effect **February 1** of the following year.

| Rating | Typical Merit Increase |
|--------|----------------------|
| 5 (Outstanding) | 7–12% |
| 4 (Exceeds Expectations) | 5–7% |
| 3 (Meets Expectations) | **3–5%** |
| 2 (Developing) | 0–2% |
| 1 (Needs Improvement) | 0% |

Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are not eligible for merit increases during the PIP period.

## 5. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

A PIP is initiated when an employee receives a rating of **2 or below in two consecutive reviews** or when performance concerns are flagged by a manager between reviews.

A PIP runs for **90 days** and includes:
- Clearly defined improvement objectives
- Weekly check-ins with the manager
- HR oversight throughout the process

Successful completion of a PIP removes the PIP status. Failure to meet PIP objectives may result in demotion or termination.

## 6. Promotions

Promotions are considered during the Annual Review cycle. Eligibility criteria:
- Must have been in current role for a minimum of **12 months**
- Must have achieved a rating of 4 or above in the most recent Annual Review
- Manager nominates the employee; Director and VP must approve

Promotion decisions and compensation adjustments are communicated simultaneously in December.

## 7. Retention of Records

Performance review records are retained for **7 years** after employment ends, in line with the Data Handling Policy.
