# NimbusWorks Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Version 1.8 | Effective: March 1, 2024**

## 1. Overview

NimbusWorks reimburses employees for reasonable and necessary business expenses incurred in the course of their work. All expenses must be pre-approved, properly documented, and submitted within the required timeframe.

## 2. Meal and Entertainment

The per diem meal allowance is:
- **Domestic travel**: $50 per day
- **International travel**: $80 per day

Business meals with clients or colleagues require the names of attendees and the business purpose to be documented. Entertainment expenses (client events, team dinners) must be pre-approved by a Director or above. There is no reimbursement for alcohol unless it is part of a pre-approved client entertainment event.

## 3. Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is reimbursable within the following limits:
- **Domestic**: up to **$200 per night**
- **International**: up to **$300 per night**

Employees should book through the company's preferred travel portal when possible. Costs above these limits require pre-approval from the department VP. Airbnb and private rentals require additional approval.

## 4. Travel

**Flights**: Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights **6 hours or longer**. Upgrades using personal frequent flyer miles are permitted without reimbursement of the upgrade difference.

**Ground transport**: Taxis, rideshares, and public transit are reimbursable. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate ($0.67 per mile as of 2024). Rental cars require prior approval.

**Train**: Standard/second class for journeys under 3 hours; first class permitted for journeys over 3 hours.

## 5. Documentation Requirements

**Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.** Expenses under $25 may be claimed without a receipt but must include a brief description. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.

All expense reports must include:
- Date and amount of expense
- Vendor name
- Business purpose
- Names of attendees (for meals and entertainment)
- Receipt image (for expenses > $25)

## 6. Submission Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within **30 days of the expense being incurred**. Late submissions may be declined unless there are exceptional circumstances approved by HR. Expenses from the previous fiscal year must be submitted by **January 15**.

## 7. Travel Advances

Employees may request a travel advance of up to **$1,000** for pre-approved trips. The advance must be reconciled against actual expenses within **15 days** of return. Unused advance funds must be returned within the same period.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable:
- Personal items (clothing, toiletries unless for travel lasting more than 5 nights)
- Speeding fines, parking tickets
- In-flight Wi-Fi (unless business-critical and pre-approved)
- Gym fees during travel
- Childcare or pet care during business travel
- Upgrades to premium economy or business class on flights under 6 hours

## 9. How to Submit

Submit all expenses through the Expensify portal linked from hr.nimbusworks.com. For questions, contact finance@nimbusworks.com.
