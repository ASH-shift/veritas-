# NimbusWorks Product Overview

**Internal Document | Last Updated: Q1 2024**

## What is NimbusWorks?

NimbusWorks is a cloud-based project management and team collaboration platform designed for modern engineering and operations teams. Launched in **2021**, NimbusWorks has grown to serve over **50,000 customers** across 60 countries.

Our platform helps teams plan, track, and deliver work faster through structured task management, automated workflows, and deep integrations with the tools teams already use.

## Core Features

### Task and Project Management
- Boards (Kanban), Lists, Gantt charts, and Calendar views
- Nested tasks (up to 5 levels deep)
- Custom fields, labels, and priority levels
- Dependency tracking between tasks

### Collaboration
- In-line comments and @mentions on tasks and documents
- Real-time multiplayer document editing
- Team wikis and knowledge bases
- Video call integration (Zoom, Google Meet)

### Automation
- 200+ built-in automation rules
- Custom workflow builder
- API webhooks for external triggers
- Zapier and Make (formerly Integromat) integrations

### Reporting
- Sprint velocity and burndown charts
- Team workload heatmaps
- Time tracking and billable hours
- Custom dashboards with drag-and-drop widgets

## Integrations

NimbusWorks integrates with over **150 tools** including:
- GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket (code commits linked to tasks)
- Slack (notifications, create tasks from messages)
- Jira (two-way sync for teams migrating from Jira)
- Salesforce, HubSpot (customer-linked projects)
- Google Drive, Dropbox, Notion (file attachments)

## Pricing Plans

| Plan | Price | Users | Storage |
|------|-------|-------|---------|
| **Free** | $0/month | Up to 5 users | 5 GB |

Enterprise plan includes: SSO/SAML, dedicated customer success manager, SLA guarantee (99.9% uptime), custom data residency options, and audit logs.

## Security and Compliance

- **SOC 2 Type II** certified (since 2022)
- **GDPR** and **CCPA** compliant
- Data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Role-based access control (RBAC) with granular permissions
- Two-factor authentication available on all plans, required on Enterprise

## SLA and Uptime

NimbusWorks guarantees **99.9% uptime** on Business and Enterprise plans. Historical uptime is published at status.nimbusworks.com. The free and starter plans do not carry an uptime SLA.
