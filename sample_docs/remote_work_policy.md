# NimbusWorks Remote Work Policy

**Version 1.4 | Effective: September 1, 2023**

## 1. Hybrid Work Model

NimbusWorks operates on a **hybrid work model**. Employees are required to be in the office a minimum of **3 days per week**. Preferred in-office days are **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday** to maximize team collaboration. Monday and Friday are optional remote days.

Exceptions to the 3-day requirement require written approval from the employee's VP. Employees in fully remote roles (as specified in their offer letter) are exempt.

## 2. Eligibility

Hybrid and remote work is available to all permanent employees who:
- Have completed their **3-month probation period**
- Have a performance rating of 3 ("Meets Expectations") or above in their most recent review
- Have a suitable home working environment with reliable internet

New employees are expected to be in the office **5 days a week** during their first 30 days of employment.

## 3. Home Office Requirements

Employees working from home must:
- Have a dedicated, private workspace free from significant distractions
- Maintain a reliable internet connection with a minimum speed of **25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload**
- Use the company VPN for all work-related tasks (see Security Policy)

The company provides a **$1,500 one-time home office equipment stipend** for remote and hybrid employees. This can be used for a desk, chair, monitor, keyboard, or other work equipment. The stipend is processed through IT and is not reimbursable via Expensify.

## 4. Internet Allowance

Remote and hybrid employees receive a **$50 per month internet allowance**. This is paid as a taxable addition to the monthly payroll. No receipt is required. Employees who move to a fully in-office role will have the allowance removed from the following month.

## 5. Core Hours

All employees, regardless of location, must be available during **core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local timezone**. Outside core hours, employees are expected to respond to messages within **4 hours** during their working day.

Employees must keep their Slack status up to date to reflect their availability.

## 6. Geographic Restrictions

For legal and tax compliance reasons, employees may not work remotely from outside their **country of employment** for more than **20 consecutive days** without prior approval from HR and Legal. Employees wishing to work internationally for an extended period must submit a Remote Work Abroad Request form at least **6 weeks in advance**.

Employees must reside within **50 miles of their assigned office** unless they are classified as fully remote in their employment contract.

## 7. Meetings and Visibility

Cameras should be on during team meetings unless the employee has a technical or personal reason. All-Hands meetings are held every other Monday and attendance (in-person or remote) is expected.

## 8. Equipment and Security

Company laptops must not be used by household members. The screen lock timeout of **5 minutes** must be maintained. See the Information Security Policy for full requirements.

If a company device is lost or stolen, report it to security@nimbusworks.com and IT at it@nimbusworks.com **immediately**.

## 9. Review of Remote Arrangements

Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually. They may be revoked if an employee's performance declines, if there are business needs requiring on-site presence, or if the employee violates this policy.
