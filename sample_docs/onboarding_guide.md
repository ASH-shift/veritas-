# NimbusWorks New Employee Onboarding Guide

**Audience: New Hires | Last Updated: February 2024**

Welcome to NimbusWorks! This guide walks you through your first 30 days. Your manager and onboarding buddy will help you navigate the process.

## Before Your First Day

Your hiring manager will send you a welcome email with:
- Laptop shipping confirmation (for remote employees)
- Badge access instructions (for office employees)
- Link to the HR portal to complete paperwork
- Slack workspace invite

Complete all pre-employment paperwork (I-9, direct deposit, emergency contacts) **before your start date**.

## Week 1: Orientation and Setup

### Day 1
- Meet your manager and team
- Set up your MacBook (IT will schedule a 1-hour onboarding call)
- Activate your Google Workspace account and set up MFA (mandatory — see Security Policy)
- Complete the Security Awareness Training module (**1 hour**, must be done within 30 days)
- Meet your **onboarding buddy** — assigned for your first 90 days

### Days 2–5
- Complete GDPR Awareness Training (**30 minutes**, required for all employees)
- Complete Code of Conduct Training (**45 minutes**, required for all employees)
- Shadow your team lead on day-to-day workflows
- Read the NimbusWorks Product Overview document
- Attend your first team standup
- Set up 1:1 with your manager (weekly cadence)

## Week 2: Product and Process Deep Dive

- Complete the full product demo walkthrough (self-paced, ~3 hours on Notion)
- Read the Engineering SOP if you are in engineering
- Join all relevant Slack channels (#general, #announcements, your team channel)
- Set up your development environment (see the Engineering Wiki on Confluence)
- Attend your first All-Hands meeting (held every other Monday at 10:00 AM Pacific)

## Weeks 3–4: First Contributions

- Begin your first project or tasks assigned by your manager
- Weekly check-in with your onboarding buddy
- Submit your first expense report if applicable (through Expensify)
- Complete the 30-day check-in meeting with HR

## The Buddy Program

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for their **first 90 days**. Your buddy is a peer (not your manager) who helps you navigate the culture, tools, and informal processes at NimbusWorks. Reach out to your buddy freely — they are there to help!

Buddy responsibilities:
- Weekly coffee chats (virtual or in-person)
- Answer informal questions about company culture
- Introduce you to other team members

## Required Training Checklist

| Training | Duration | Deadline |
