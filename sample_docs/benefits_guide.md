# NimbusWorks Employee Benefits Guide

**Effective: January 1, 2024 | US Employees**

## 1. Health Insurance

NimbusWorks provides comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage through Aetna.

**Medical Insurance**
- NimbusWorks pays **80%** of the monthly premium; employee pays **20%**
- Plans offered: PPO (preferred), HDHP, and HMO
- Coverage begins on the **first day of employment** (no waiting period)
- Dependents (spouse, domestic partner, children under 26) can be added at enrollment

**Dental Insurance**
- NimbusWorks pays **70%** of the monthly premium; employee pays **30%**
- Covers preventive care (100%), basic procedures (80%), and major procedures (50%)
- Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per person

**Vision Insurance**
- NimbusWorks pays 100% of the premium for employee only coverage
- Annual eye exam covered in full; $200 allowance for frames/lenses or contact lenses

## 2. Retirement — 401(k) Plan

NimbusWorks offers a 401(k) plan through Fidelity.

- **Company match**: NimbusWorks matches **100% of employee contributions up to 4%** of gross salary
- Example: if you earn $100,000/year and contribute 4% ($4,000), NimbusWorks adds $4,000
- Vesting schedule: **immediate** — company match vests on day one
- Employees are automatically enrolled at 3% contribution rate; you may change this anytime
- Roth 401(k) option is also available

## 3. Gym and Wellness

- **Gym allowance**: $50 per month reimbursable for gym membership, fitness apps, or wellness equipment
- Submit receipts through Expensify with category "Wellness Benefit"
- Eligible: gym memberships, Peloton, yoga studios, running apps (Strava, Nike Run Club premium)
- Not eligible: sports equipment over $100/item, sports team fees

## 4. Learning and Development

- **Annual learning budget**: **$1,000 per employee per year**
- Eligible: online courses (Coursera, Udemy, Pluralsight), books, conferences, certifications
- Pre-approval required for expenses over $200
- Unused budget does not roll over to the next year
- Submit through Expensify with category "Learning & Development"

## 5. Life Insurance and Disability

- **Life insurance**: NimbusWorks provides basic coverage of **3× your annual base salary** at no cost
- Additional voluntary coverage up to 5× salary can be purchased at employee's cost
- **Short-term disability**: 60% of base salary for up to 12 weeks
- **Long-term disability**: 60% of base salary after 90 days, up to age 65

## 6. Parental Leave

- **Maternity leave**: 26 weeks paid (see Leave Policy for full details)
- **Paternity leave**: 2 weeks paid (see Leave Policy)
- Primary caregiver additional unpaid leave: up to 4 weeks with approval
- NimbusWorks provides a $500 new parent gift card upon the birth or adoption of a child

## 7. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

All employees and their household members have access to the EAP through ComPsych:
- **6 free counseling sessions** per issue per year
- Legal and financial consultation
- Available 24/7 by phone: 1-800-555-0199

## 8. Commuter Benefits

- Pre-tax commuter benefits up to $315/month for transit and parking (IRS 2024 limit)
- Managed through WageWorks

## 9. Other Perks

| Home office setup | $1,500 one-time stipend (remote employees) |

## 10. Benefits Enrollment

Open enrollment occurs each year in **November** for the following calendar year. New hires have **30 days from their start date** to enroll. Changes outside open enrollment are only permitted following a Qualifying Life Event (marriage, divorce, birth of a child, etc.).

For questions, contact benefits@nimbusworks.com or the benefits portal at benefits.nimbusworks.com.
